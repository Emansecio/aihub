## Prompt utilizado

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a finished Windows desktop application icon for AIHub, a small native radial AI launcher that docks at the edge of the monitor. Create ONE square app icon, front-facing and orthographic, centered, large and perfectly legible at small sizes. The mark should visually echo a luxurious black rotary knob: a bold circular graphite dial with a restrained silver bevel, a luminous violet indicator dot, and a thick smoothly curved violet orbital arc embracing the right side of the dial. The arc symbolizes selecting apps by scrolling. Dark charcoal, silver and amethyst purple palette, subtle dimensional shading, crisp minimal silhouette, professional desktop software identity. Transparent background outside the icon silhouette, real alpha transparency, no checkerboard. Fill about 85 percent of the square with the mark, centered balanced composition. No words, no letters, no AI acronym, no third-party logos, no app mockups, no tiny decoration, no perspective tilt, no multiple concepts, no surrounding square tile. Make the violet dot and crescent substantial enough to read at 32px. Deliver a high-quality square PNG suitable as the source for a Windows ICO.
